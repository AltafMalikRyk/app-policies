Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 | Application: VacuumCleanerSimulator

1. Overview & Simulation Disclaimer

VacuumCleanerSimulator (Package Name: com.datafaiq.vacuumcleanersimulator) is developed purely as an offline entertainment, ASMR soundscape, and soothing white noise relaxation utility. The application is designed to simulate the sound, frequency vibrations, and visual aesthetics of an electric vacuum cleaner to help users relax, concentrate, meditate, or soothe crying infants.

Important Entertainment & Simulation Notice: VacuumCleanerSimulator is strictly an audio-visual software simulation. It does not perform physical cleaning, generate actual air suction, or interface with real-world home appliances.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe in uncompromising privacy. VacuumCleanerSimulator is engineered from the ground up to operate completely self-contained.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Device Information: We do not harvest IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, IP addresses, advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware serials.
  • No Sensor or Audio Recordings: Although the app produces soothing audio white noise, it never accesses the microphone or records ambient sound.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request, track, or process GPS coordinates or network-derived location data.
Summary of Data Collection: ZERO bytes of user or device data are collected, stored, transmitted, or sold by VacuumCleanerSimulator.

3. Zero Runtime & Sensitive Device Permissions

VacuumCleanerSimulator requires ZERO runtime or sensitive Android device permissions.

  • No Camera or Photo Library access.
  • No Microphone or Audio Recording permissions.
  • No Shared Storage, Photos, Media, or External Files access.
  • No Location (Fine or Coarse) permissions.
  • No Contacts, Phone, SMS, or Calendar access.
  • The standard install-time tactile vibration feature (if supported by your hardware) is used exclusively for localized motor rumble simulation while the app is active and can be toggled off at any time.

4. 100% Offline & Zero Third-Party Tracking

VacuumCleanerSimulator is a strictly offline Android application. It does not contain any:

  • Third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel).
  • Third-party advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
  • Behavioral tracking or user profiling scripts.
  • External remote servers or cloud databases.

All application logic, sound synthesis assets, animations, and preferences (such as volume level and motor pitch) remain entirely on your local device.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because vacuum white noise is frequently used by parents as a gentle sleep aid for infants and toddlers, child safety is paramount. VacuumCleanerSimulator is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K):

  • The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
  • No personalized tracking, behavioral ads, or in-app purchases are present.
  • The application is safe for families and children of all ages.

6. Data Retention & Local Preferences

Any user-selected settings (e.g., continuous rumble toggle, sleep timer selection, or motor pitch) are stored strictly within the app's local memory or private sandboxed preferences on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local configuration data.
