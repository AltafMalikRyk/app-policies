Privacy Policy

DiscountCalc Tool (com.articode.app.discountcalctool)
100% Offline • Zero Permissions • Zero Tracking

1. Zero Data Collection

DiscountCalc Tool is designed with privacy as an absolute guarantee. We do not collect, store, transmit, rent, sell, or share any personal information, usage telemetry, analytics, crash diagnostics, or device identifiers. No user account or registration is ever required.

2. Zero Permissions Architecture

DiscountCalc Tool operates under a strict zero-permission Android manifest architecture:

  • No access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS.
  • No access to Precise or Coarse Location, Bluetooth, or Wi-Fi scanning.
  • No access to External Storage or Media Libraries.
  • Zero dangerous runtime permission prompts will ever appear.

3. Fully Offline Client-Side Processing

All shopping discount, coupon stacking, tax computation, and bill-splitting math happens instantaneously inside volatile local device memory. Only your display preferences (Dark/Light theme, numeric decimal precision, and haptic preference) are saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and are permanently deleted upon uninstall.

4. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Because DiscountCalc Tool collects zero personal data, runs completely offline, and contains no third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs, it strictly complies with COPPA and global child privacy protection standards and is safe for all ages.
