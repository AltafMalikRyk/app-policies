⚠️ Important Medical & Simulation Notice

ThermometerSimulator is strictly an entertainment and casual visual simulation application. It is NOT a medical device, clinical diagnostic tool, or physical temperature sensor. Consumer smartphones lack the certified hardware sensors necessary to measure human body temperature. Never rely on this application for medical diagnosis, clinical evaluations, fever assessment, or health decisions. Always consult a licensed physician and utilize a medically certified thermometer for clinical thermal readings.

2. Zero Personal & Health Data Collection

ThermometerSimulator does not gather or track any personal or sensitive user information. Specifically:

  • No Health or Biometric Information: The app does not read, record, or sync any biological, medical, or physiological health data.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
  • No Location Data: The app does not access GPS, Wi-Fi location, or IP-based geolocation.
  • No Identifiers: We do not log Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or unique device tokens.

3. Zero Device Permissions

ThermometerSimulator requests zero sensitive runtime permissions in the Android operating system. The application functions entirely through standard Flutter user interface rendering:

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Location or Bluetooth access.
  • No Body Sensors or Physical Activity access.
  • No Read/Write External Storage access.

4. No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising Networks

ThermometerSimulator does not embed any third-party analytics trackers, advertising software development kits (SDKs), or user tracking beacons (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or AdMob). The application is 100% ad-free and tracker-free.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because ThermometerSimulator does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, it fully complies with the United States Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global child privacy regulations (including GDPR-K). Children can safely enjoy the interactive fluid physics and gauge demonstrations without privacy risk.

6. Security of Your Device

All user preference toggles (such as sound mute, haptics, and temperature scale selection) are held transiently in application memory during your active session. No persistent remote logs or user profiles are ever created or maintained.
