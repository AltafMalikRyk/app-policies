Privacy Policy - StampSimulator

Effective Date: October 2026 | Application: StampSimulator (com.datafaiq.stampsimulator)

Quick Summary: StampSimulator is an offline, standalone casual entertainment simulation app. It collects zero personal data, requests zero runtime device permissions, uses no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and does not connect to external servers.

1. Entertainment Simulation Disclaimer

StampSimulator is created exclusively for casual entertainment, office roleplay, stress relief, and satisfying tactile/audio ASMR simulation. All seals ("APPROVED", "REJECTED", "TOP SECRET", "PAID", "CONFIDENTIAL", "VERIFIED") and paper documents simulated inside the app are entirely fictional visual representations and hold no legal, financial, governmental, or official validity whatsoever.

2. Zero Data Collection Policy

We believe in absolute user privacy. When you download and use StampSimulator:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, gather, or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Location Data: The application does not access GPS, Wi-Fi location, or cellular network location.
  • No Device Files or Media Access: The app never requests access to your photos, camera, microphone, internal files, or external storage.
  • No Keystroke or Input Logging: Your virtual stamp positions and paper canvas drawings exist purely in temporary device memory (RAM) while the app is active and are completely erased upon reset or exit.

3. Zero Sensitive Device Permissions

StampSimulator is architected to be completely self-contained. The application does not request nor utilize any Android runtime permissions:

  • android.permission.CAMERA - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE - NOT REQUESTED

Haptic vibration feedback utilizes standard Android non-sensitive hardware vibration APIs solely to provide satisfying tactile feedback when a stamp slams against the paper.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

StampSimulator contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network) and no analytics trackers (such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust, or AppsFlyer). Your activity remains strictly on your personal device.

5. Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Because StampSimulator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from any user, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. It is safe for audiences of all ages, including children under 13.

6. Security and Data Retention

Because no user data is collected or transmitted over any network, there is no transmission risk or server-side data retention. All interactive stamping states are volatile and reset when you clear the document or close the app.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory adjustments. Any future modifications will be posted directly to this public webpage with an updated effective date.
