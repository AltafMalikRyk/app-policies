Privacy Policy

Electric Drill Simulator • com.datafaiq.electricdrillsimulator

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines the privacy practices of the development team for Electric Drill Simulator ("we," "our," or "the application"). We respect your fundamental right to privacy and are committed to ensuring a secure and transparent user experience.

Electric Drill Simulator is designed as an interactive offline simulation application for entertainment, sound effects, workshop ASMR roleplay, and novelty purposes. The application operates completely client-side on your device without transmitting, processing, or sharing your data with external servers.

2. Information Collection and Use (Zero Personal Data)

We collect zero personally identifiable information (PII).

Electric Drill Simulator does NOT collect, store, track, monitor, or transmit any user information whatsoever. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details
  • Precise or coarse geographic location data (GPS, IP, Wi-Fi)
  • Unique device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Advertising IDs)
  • Photos, media files, documents, or personal storage contents
  • Financial or payment transaction details
  • Behavioral analytics, telemetry, or app usage monitoring

User preferences configured within the tool (such as selected drill bit, torque clutch level, gear speed, or sound volume) are maintained temporarily in device memory or local app storage solely to preserve your session settings. This data never leaves your hardware.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Electric Drill Simulator operates with absolute transparency and requires zero dangerous runtime permissions. The only hardware interaction requested is:

  • VIBRATE Permission: Used exclusively to trigger the device's built-in haptic vibrator motor when depressing the drill trigger button, delivering realistic mechanical feedback. Users may disable haptic feedback at any time via in-app settings.
  • Audio System: The app outputs procedurally generated audio waves through standard system audio channels via the Android SoundPool API. It does not record audio or access your microphone.

The app does NOT request camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, phone status, or external storage permissions.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Electric Drill Simulator does not embed any third-party SDKs, tracking frameworks, behavioral analytics tools, or advertising networks. We do not use Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or any external telemetry libraries. The application is completely self-contained.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

Because Electric Drill Simulator is designed for family-friendly entertainment, ASMR satisfaction, and workshop demonstration, we strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

We do not knowingly collect, process, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Since the app collects no data from any user, children can enjoy the simulation in complete safety.

6. Security & Offline Architecture

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is zero risk of server-side data interception, unauthorized exposure, or data breach. All core features function completely offline without an active internet connection.

7. Policy Updates & Modifications

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application updates or regulatory revisions. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of the application following the posting of updates constitutes acceptance of those changes.

8. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with applicable global data privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
