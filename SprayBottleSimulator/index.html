Official Privacy Policy & Safety Standard

Spray Bottle Simulator

Effective Date: October 2026 • Application ID: com.datafaiq.spraybottlesimulator • Platform: Android

Privacy Summary: Spray Bottle Simulator is a 100% offline, privacy-first sensory ASMR entertainment application. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information or device telemetry. Zero device permissions and zero internet connectivity are required to use the application.

1. Sensory Entertainment & ASMR Simulation Disclaimer

Spray Bottle Simulator is developed solely for casual digital simulation, sensory stress relief, auditory ASMR relaxation, and playful virtual plant watering roleplay. All spray nozzle animations, lever compression physics, mechanical pump squeaks, water hiss soundscapes, and glass misting droplets are digital simulations generated locally on your device. No physical liquids or aerosols are dispensed or consumed.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe digital entertainment should never compromise personal privacy. When you download, install, and interact with Spray Bottle Simulator:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or social media accounts.
  • No Hardware Identifiers: We do not log IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware serial fingerprints.
  • No Activity Telemetry: We do not monitor how frequently you spray, your selected nozzle settings, or your in-app session duration.

3. Zero Runtime or Sensitive Device Permissions

Spray Bottle Simulator operates completely self-contained. The application requests zero dangerous or sensitive permissions from your Android operating system:

  • No Camera or Video Recording permissions.
  • No Microphone or Audio Recording permissions.
  • No Precise or Approximate Location (GPS/Network) permissions.
  • No Read/Write External Storage or Photos/Media library permissions.
  • No Contacts, Call Logs, or Phone State access.

Audio playback is handled through standard Android media components and tactile haptic recoil pulses utilize the standard device vibrator API without reading or accessing any user data.

4. No Third-Party Tracking & 100% Offline Operation

The application is designed to function entirely offline without external network dependencies:

  • No Analytics SDKs: No third-party behavioral analytics or usage trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) are bundled.
  • No Advertisements: There are no ad networks, banner ads, interstitial popups, or marketing trackers embedded in the software.
  • Zero Background Transmission: The app never transmits device data or background payloads to external servers.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Friendly)

Spray Bottle Simulator complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global child safety standards. Because the app does not collect, retain, or share any personal data whatsoever, it is completely safe and appropriate for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

6. Local Preference Storage

Any configuration preferences you adjust inside the app (such as sound mute toggles, haptics preferences, mist spread calibration, or liquid tint color) are stored exclusively on your device using standard Android local sandboxing. This data never leaves your device and is completely removed if you uninstall the application.

7. Policy Updates & Modifications

We may periodically review and update this Privacy Policy to reflect future features or updates to the application. Any changes will be published immediately to this official URL with an updated effective date.
