SpiritLevelSimulator Privacy Policy

App: SpiritLevelSimulator Package: com.datafaiq.spiritlevelsimulator Effective Date: October 2026

Our Unconditional Privacy Commitment

SpiritLevelSimulator is a 100% offline, self-contained casual carpentry and bubble balance simulation app. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or monetize any personal data, device identifiers, or sensor telemetry. No account is required, and zero runtime device permissions are requested.

1 Entertainment & Casual Simulation Disclaimer

SpiritLevelSimulator is engineered strictly for casual entertainment, leisure, DIY roleplay, visual gratification, and desk fidgeting. It simulates the aesthetic physics, fluid inertia, and acoustics of traditional spirit levels. This application is intended purely for amusement and is not certified for industrial surveying, commercial construction, or critical architectural leveling.

2 Zero Personal Data Collection

We respect your privacy unconditionally. SpiritLevelSimulator does not gather, transmit, or process any identifiable personal information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical mailing addresses.
  • Device hardware IDs, advertising identifiers (AAID/IDFA), MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
  • Biometric information, sensor telemetry recordings, or touch gesture history.
  • User keystrokes, clipboard records, or lists of installed applications.
  • Geographic location data (neither coarse network location nor fine GPS location is requested or accessible).

3 Zero Sensitive or Runtime Device Permissions

The application is designed to be completely self-contained. The Android Manifest requires ZERO runtime or sensitive permissions.

No Camera or Microphone

Zero audio or visual recording hardware is ever initialized or accessed.

No Storage Access

No access to your personal files, gallery, photos, or media storage.

No Location Services

Zero tracking of geographic coordinates, Wi-Fi SSIDs, or network cell towers.

No Contacts or Accounts

Zero access to personal address books, social profiles, or device identity.

4 Local Audio Chime & Tactile Haptic Feedback

To provide realistic tactile satisfaction and carpenter tool feedback, the application utilizes standard local Android system capabilities:

  • Acoustic Chime: When the bubble aligns dead-center at 0.0°, a metallic alignment ding tone is synthesized locally using Android system audio services. No audio is ever captured, analyzed, or uploaded.
  • Tactile Haptics: A subtle vibration pulse provides sensory confirmation when the tool snaps into balance. Haptic feedback is executed strictly on the physical device hardware and can be easily toggled on or off in the Settings menu at any time.

5 No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising SDKs

SpiritLevelSimulator does not embed any third-party analytics libraries, advertising networks, tracking beacons, or telemetry SDKs. There are no background data transmissions, invisible pixels, or user profiling algorithms.

6 Child Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because SpiritLevelSimulator does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Family Policy requirements. The app is completely safe, benign, and suitable for users of all ages, including children and families.

7 Local Application Preferences

Any visual settings you select—such as the carpenter body theme (Classic Mahogany Wood, Heavy Cast Iron, Hi-Vis Yellow) and fluid vial color (Neon Green, Electric Cyan, Amber Gold)—are kept strictly in local memory on your device. This data never leaves your device and is erased immediately if you uninstall the application.
