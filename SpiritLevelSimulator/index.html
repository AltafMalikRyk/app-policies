ℹ️ 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Spirit Level Simulator. This application is an interactive audio-visual simulation designed purely for carpenter bubble vial physics appreciation, tactile alignment satisfaction, and casual DIY tool enjoyment.

We respect your privacy completely. Spirit Level Simulator operates 100% locally on your device with no network communication, no background tracking, and zero personal data collection.

🛡️ 2. Information We Collect

Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, process, harvest, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever. Specifically, we do not access:

  • User identity, real name, email, telephone number, or postal address.
  • Device serial numbers, IMEI, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware parameters.
  • Geographic locations, GPS coordinates, or approximate network geolocation.
  • Camera imagery, microphone recordings, photo galleries, or local storage documents.
  • Sensor logs, calibration history, contacts, or browsing history.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Information

Because zero user data is ever accessed, collected, or stored, no personal information is used, evaluated, commercialized, or shared. All user preferences—such as selected beam themes, bubble sensitivity, and volume settings—exist solely in local runtime memory during your active session.

📱 4. Device Permissions

Spirit Level Simulator requests no sensitive or dangerous Android runtime permissions. The only permission requested is:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to generate tactile vibration pulses when the bubble locks at true 0.0° center level. This permission cannot read private user files or connect to network services.

🚫 5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Spirit Level Simulator does NOT include any third-party analytics libraries, advertising frameworks, telemetry tools, or tracking SDKs. There is zero integration with platforms such as AdMob, Unity Ads, Firebase Analytics, or data brokers.

👶 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Spirit Level Simulator is safe, educational, and fun for users of all age groups, including children under 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K regulations, and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not solicit or gather data from minors.

🔒 7. Data Security & Storage

Because the app contains no remote cloud servers or data transmission sockets, your mobile device remains fully isolated and secure while using the simulation.
