Privacy Policy for Tape Dispenser Simulator

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 | Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Tape Dispenser Simulator ("the Application"), developed by independent mobile developers as an offline, interactive packaging tape peeling simulation and ASMR soundboard. The application is built solely for casual amusement, tactile relaxation, sensory stress relief, and harmless fun.

Entertainment Declaration: Tape Dispenser Simulator is an artistic audio-visual simulation utility. It produces simulated animations and soundscapes only and carries zero physical tape or blade cutting hazards.

We are dedicated to safeguarding user privacy. This Privacy Policy details our strict adherence to zero personal data gathering, safe on-device processing, and complete transparency.

2. Information We Collect

Tape Dispenser Simulator does NOT collect, record, process, transmit, or retain any personal information.

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request or gather user names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, billing details, or physical locations.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not read or record IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: The application does not possess or request location-sensing capabilities; GPS and network-based location are neither accessed nor tracked.
  • No Storage Access: The app does not read, modify, or upload device storage, gallery photos, videos, or external files.

3. How We Use Information (Offline Execution)

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is zero data to process, sell, monetize, or distribute:

  • 100% Offline-First Architecture: All simulation code, procedural audio synthesis, physics engines, and graphical assets are self-contained within the application package. No internet connection is required for full functionality.
  • Zero Analytics & Telemetry: The application incorporates zero telemetry trackers, behavioral event loggers, screen monitors, or analytics SDKs.
  • Zero Remote Servers: The application never establishes socket connections or transmits HTTP/HTTPS traffic to remote servers.

4. Device Permissions

Tape Dispenser Simulator requires no sensitive runtime permissions. It relies solely on standard non-sensitive hardware features:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): A normal-level permission used exclusively to trigger tactile adhesive peeling micro-vibrations and blade cut snap recoils via the device haptic motor when pulling or cutting tape.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Tape Dispenser Simulator contains NO third-party advertising networks, commercial profiling engines, trackers, or data brokers. Users will never encounter banner ads, rewarded video popups, or commercial tracking libraries. We never share or sell user information.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Tape Dispenser Simulator is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy Requirements. Because our app contains zero data collection, zero targeted advertising, and safe sensory-themed content, it is appropriate for audiences of all ages, including children and families.

7. Data Security & Storage

The application operates entirely within ephemeral memory during user sessions. User settings (such as chosen tape material, haptic state, and audio volume) are saved strictly to local app preferences. No sensitive credentials or remote databases exist. The application adheres to strict Android sandboxing standards.

8. Policy Changes & Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to comply with evolving Android platform requirements or legal guidelines. Any revisions will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of any updated terms.
