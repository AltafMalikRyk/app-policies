Privacy Policy

IdiomPulse Dictionary (com.articode.app.idiompulsedictionary)
100% Offline • Complete Handbook • Zero Permissions

1. Zero Data Collection

IdiomPulse Dictionary collects zero personal data, search inputs, saved idiom lists, usage telemetry, crash logs, or advertising identifiers. Nothing you browse, copy, or bookmark leaves your device.

2. Zero Permissions

IdiomPulse Dictionary requests zero dangerous or runtime Android permissions:

  • No access to Storage, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or Location.
  • No internet sockets, remote analytics scripts, third-party ad SDKs, or background tracking services.

3. 100% Client-Side Offline Utility

All English idioms, plain definitions, historical etymologies, and example sentences are compiled directly into offline app assets. Bookmarked entries and Light/Sepia/Dark theme preferences live exclusively in local Android SharedPreferences and vanish cleanly on uninstall.

4. Age Rating & Policy

IdiomPulse Dictionary is tailored for general language and reference readers (13+ / 18+), gathers zero data from users of any age, and complies strictly with COPPA and Google Play privacy standards.
