Privacy Policy for Water Tap Simulator

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026
Privacy Summary: Water Tap Simulator is an offline casual entertainment and relaxation app. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, identifiers, or location information. It requires zero sensitive device permissions.

1. Purpose and Scope

Water Tap Simulator is designed and provided purely for casual entertainment, fun interactivity, stress relief, and acoustic water flow relaxation. The application provides interactive faucet (tooti) controls, realistic water flow dynamics (dripping, steady flow, cascading stream), splash particle effects, and soothing natural flowing water sound effects. We respect your digital privacy, and this document explains our clear and transparent privacy practices.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Water Tap Simulator does not collect, record, log, monitor, process, transmit, or share any personal information whatsoever.

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request, access, or store your name, email address, postal address, phone number, contacts, or accounts.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect hardware serial numbers, IMEI codes, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
  • No Location Data: The application does not access or determine precise GPS location or coarse network-based geographical data.
  • No Media or File Access: The app does not read, write, or access your camera, microphone, internal storage, external SD cards, photos, or documents.

3. Device Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

Water Tap Simulator is engineered as a standalone, self-contained offline application with zero runtime permissions:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: No dangerous or sensitive Android permissions are requested in the manifest or during runtime (No Camera, Microphone, Storage, Location, Contacts, or Phone State).
  • Completely Offline Operation: All graphic illustrations, procedural fluid animations, particle physics, and sound effects are embedded directly in the application package. No internet connection or network data plan is required to use the simulator.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

Water Tap Simulator contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, profiling trackers, or telemetry analytics tools. We do not integrate ad networks, behavioral tracking tools, or cloud telemetry. We never sell, lease, or share any user metrics with data brokers, marketing agencies, or third parties.

5. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

Water Tap Simulator complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the app displays zero advertisements, collects zero personal information, requires no device permissions, and features friendly, harmless visual faucet simulation, it is suitable and safe for users of all ages, including children and families.

6. Security & Data Safety

Because Water Tap Simulator operates completely offline on your device without communicating with external web servers or cloud databases, there is no risk of cloud data breaches, eavesdropping, or unauthorized data exposure. The application runs securely within Android's protected application sandbox.
