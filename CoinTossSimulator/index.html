1 Overview & App Purpose

CoinTossSimulator ("the Application") is developed as a lightweight, tactile entertainment simulation and fair 50/50 decision-making aid. It provides realistic 3D metallic coin flipping physics, authentic acoustic resonance sound simulation, and multi-coin skin customization for sports toss decisions (e.g. cricket, soccer), board games, casual debates, and fidget entertainment.

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict privacy commitment and explains how your information is handled—which is by not collecting any information at all.
