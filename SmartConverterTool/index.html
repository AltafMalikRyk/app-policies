Privacy Policy

SmartConverter Tool (com.articode.app.smartconvertertool)
100% Offline • Zero Permissions • Zero Tracking

1. Zero Data Collection

SmartConverter Tool collects zero personal data, conversion histories, telemetry, device identifiers, or analytics. No login or user account exists.

2. Zero Permissions Architecture

SmartConverter Tool requests zero Android runtime or dangerous permissions:

  • No access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Location.
  • No access to External Storage or Media Files.
  • Zero embedded advertising frameworks or tracking SDKs.

3. Fully Offline Processing

All unit conversions execute completely offline inside volatile device memory. Local UI settings (Dark/Light theme, haptic feedback, decimal precision) remain strictly on your device in SharedPreferences and are permanently removed on uninstall.

4. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

SmartConverter Tool complies with COPPA and global privacy laws because it collects zero personal information and is safe for users of all ages.
