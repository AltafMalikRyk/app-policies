Privacy Policy for MagnetSimulator

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 | Last Updated: October 14, 2026 | Application: MagnetSimulator (com.datafaiq.magnetsimulator)
🛡️ Direct Privacy Guarantee

MagnetSimulator is an entirely standalone, offline entertainment and physics simulation application. It does NOT collect, track, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or biometric information. It requests zero runtime permissions and operates without external network connections.

1. Entertainment & Physics Simulation Scope

MagnetSimulator is developed strictly for casual entertainment, physics demonstrations, stress relief, and educational curiosity. The application simulates the tactile behavior of magnetic dipoles (North and South poles), interactive magnetic field vector flux lines, iron filings alignment dynamics, and metal sphere capture physics.

The magnetic forces simulated within the app are mathematical models computed purely through touch coordinates and visual rendering. The application does not require or interface with sensitive hardware magnetometers or internal compass sensors for its operation.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe that entertainment utilities should never compromise your digital privacy. MagnetSimulator does not gather or process any user data whatsoever:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, home address, phone number, contacts, or billing records.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not log IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, IP addresses, or serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: The application possesses no capability to read precise GPS coordinates or coarse network geolocation.
  • No Media or File Access: The application does not access your camera, microphone, internal photo gallery, document storage, or external SD storage.

3. Zero Runtime Permissions & Completely Offline Architecture

MagnetSimulator has been engineered to adhere to the highest standard of self-containment:

  • Zero Android Permissions: The application manifest declares no dangerous, sensitive, or special access permissions. It does not request runtime permissions for camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage.
  • 100% Offline Standalone Operation: All graphic assets, procedural physics algorithms, canvas renderers, and acoustic audio triggers are bundled directly inside the local application package. The application never makes outbound HTTP/HTTPS network requests, API calls, or cloud synchronizations.

4. No Third-Party Tracking, Analytics, or Advertising

MagnetSimulator contains NO third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising networks, telemetry profiling, or behavioral analytics:

  • No banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, or rewarded video advertisements are shown.
  • No third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Adjust, or AppsFlyer) are included.
  • We never monetize, sell, lease, or distribute user metrics to data aggregators or marketing partners.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) & Families Compliance

MagnetSimulator is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • The application does not collect any data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in any jurisdiction).
  • The content consists solely of benign, harmless, and educational physics simulation suitable and safe for audiences of all age groups.
  • No external links, social logins, or chat capabilities are present within the simulation environment.

6. Device Security & Operating System Sandbox

MagnetSimulator executes strictly within the standard Android application sandbox. Because it does not exchange data with external web servers, there is zero risk of remote data interception, man-in-the-middle exploits, or cloud database breaches.
