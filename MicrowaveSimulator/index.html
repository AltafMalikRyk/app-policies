Privacy Policy for Microwave Simulator

Privacy Commitment Summary: Microwave Simulator is a 100% offline casual entertainment and acoustic appliance white noise simulation utility. It does NOT collect, store, log, transmit, or share any personal data, location records, or device identifiers. It requires zero sensitive device permissions and contains zero tracking mechanisms.

1. Purpose and Scope of Application

Microwave Simulator is distributed exclusively as an offline casual entertainment and acoustic white noise utility. It allows users of all ages to enjoy realistic kitchen microwave oven visual dynamics (including rotating glass turntables, illuminated heating chambers, digital countdown timers, and simulated food items like popcorn and pizza) alongside soothing magnetron motor hums and finish beeps designed for kitchen roleplay, stress relief, and acoustic background relaxation. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting user privacy.

2. Entertainment and White Noise Disclaimer

Microwave Simulator is created purely for entertainment, culinary pretend play, and acoustic background sound simulation. While many individuals find rhythmic mechanical white noise beneficial for masking distracting environmental sounds and improving concentration, the application does not claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical sleep disorder or condition. Users should adjust audio volume to a safe and comfortable listening level.

3. Zero Personal Data Collection

Microwave Simulator strictly collects NO personal or non-personal data from its users:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request, access, collect, or store your name, email address, physical location, telephone number, user accounts, or contacts.
  • No Hardware or Network Identifiers: We do not collect device IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or IP addresses.
  • No Geographic Location Access: The application does not request or track precise GPS coordinates or coarse cellular/Wi-Fi location data.
  • No Media, Camera, or File Access: The app has zero access to your photos, media library, internal storage, microphone, or camera.

4. Zero Runtime Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

The application is engineered as a self-contained, offline Android application:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: No runtime permissions are requested or declared in the Android manifest. The app operates safely within the standard Android application sandbox without needing elevated system access.
  • 100% Standalone Operation: All graphic assets, procedural animations, audio loops, and simulation mechanics are bundled directly inside the local application package (.apk/.aab). An active internet connection or cellular data connection is not required to operate the simulator.

5. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Telemetry

Microwave Simulator incorporates NO third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or remote telemetry frameworks. We do not partner with data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics providers. No information is transmitted to external servers or cloud repositories.

6. Children's Online Privacy & Family Safety (COPPA Compliance)

Microwave Simulator complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements. Because our app contains zero advertisements, collects zero personal information, requires zero device permissions, and features harmless visual simulations suitable for all age groups, it is completely safe and appropriate for children and families.

7. Security and Sandboxing

Because the app functions strictly offline and does not transmit data over the internet, your device is fully protected from data eavesdropping, man-in-the-middle attacks, and remote data breaches. All settings and preferences are stored locally in private application sandbox storage and are completely erased if the user uninstalls the application.
