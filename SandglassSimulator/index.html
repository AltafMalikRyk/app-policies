✦ Quick Privacy Highlights

Sandglass Simulator is an entirely offline entertainment and zen focus timer. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, usage metrics, or device identifiers. There are no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and zero runtime device permissions required.

1. Nature of the Application & Zen Disclaimer

Sandglass Simulator is a casual entertainment and mindful relaxation simulation designed to replicate the visual beauty and ambient soothing acoustics of an antique glass hourglass. The falling sand particle physics, dynamic mound accumulation, and trickling audio are created purely for artistic aesthetics, visual meditation, desk decoration, and casual interval timing.

This software does not offer medical treatment, certified psychiatric therapies, or industrial timing precision. It is built solely for calming leisure, concentration assistance, and recreational enjoyment.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We respect your privacy as an absolute priority. When you install and use Sandglass Simulator:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or financial details.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not log IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware footprints.
  • No Precise or Coarse Location: We do not access GPS, network location, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.
  • No Cloud Storage or Telemetry: No data is ever transmitted from your device to external servers or third-party databases.

3. Device Permissions & System Access

Sandglass Simulator has been engineered under a strict zero-privilege model:

  • Zero Runtime Permissions: The application does NOT request any dangerous or sensitive runtime Android permissions (such as CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, READ_CONTACTS, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • Normal Haptic Feedback: The application may access the device's tactile vibration motor solely to provide gentle physical feedback when you interactively flip the hourglass. This action occurs exclusively on your local device hardware and does not expose any personal data.
  • Zero Internet Transmission: The application does not maintain network listeners or background polling tasks.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

Sandglass Simulator is completely clean and untracked:

  • We do NOT integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or third-party telemetry tools.
  • We do NOT host third-party ad networks, banners, interstitials, or tracking beacons.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade user data to any data brokers or commercial organizations.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Sandglass Simulator does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone of any age, it is inherently compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and Google Play Families Policy Requirements. Parents and guardians can confidently allow children to enjoy the peaceful visual simulation without privacy concerns.

6. User Control & Data Retention

All user preferences (such as sound toggle, haptic vibration toggle, selected sand color theme, and timer duration presets) are stored strictly locally on your device storage. If you choose to uninstall the application, all locally saved preferences are automatically and permanently deleted by the Android operating system.

7. Security & Offline Reliability

By operating 100% offline without remote network communication, the application minimizes cybersecurity attack surfaces. Your device stays cool, secure, and private at all times.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect any regulatory evolutions or updates in platform compliance. Any amendments will be published directly to this public policy page with an updated revision date.
