ℹ️ 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Zipper Simulator. This application is an interactive audio-visual recreation designed purely for mechanical simulation, tactile fidgeting, and sensory satisfaction, replicating the mechanics, acoustics, and friction of jacket metal zippers.

We respect your privacy unconditionally. Zipper Simulator operates completely offline on your device and does not monitor, track, store, or transmit any user data or device telemetry.

🛡️ 2. Information We Collect

Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, process, harvest, store, or transmit any personal information whatsoever. Specifically, we do not access:

  • User identity, name, date of birth, postal address, or contact details.
  • Device serial numbers, IMEI, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware identifiers.
  • Geographic locations, GPS coordinates, or IP-based network geolocation.
  • Camera, microphone, phone state, photo library, or local storage documents.
  • Social media profiles, contacts, browsing history, or payment credentials.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Information

Because zero personal information is gathered, no user data is ever processed, analyzed, commercialized, or shared. All user preferences—such as chosen zipper metal finishes, fabric backgrounds, and volume settings—are maintained purely in transient local memory during runtime.

📱 4. Device Permissions

Zipper Simulator requires no sensitive or invasive Android runtime permissions. The only permission declared is:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to trigger subtle haptic pulses when the virtual zipper teeth interlock. This permission cannot access personal files or network interfaces.

🚫 5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Zipper Simulator contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, telemetry libraries, or data broker links. There are zero integrations with platforms such as AdMob, Unity, Firebase Telemetry, or Meta Audience Network.

👶 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Zipper Simulator is safe and suitable for users of all age brackets, including children under 13. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K regulations, and Google Play's Families Policy. No personal data is knowingly requested from any child.

🔒 7. Data Security & Storage

Because the app contains no network sockets, backend servers, or cloud storage, your mobile device remains fully isolated from data leakage risks. The application code executes cleanly in a local sandbox.

We may occasionally revise this Privacy Policy to ensure alignment with future platform updates or legal standards. Any revisions will be published immediately to this URL.
