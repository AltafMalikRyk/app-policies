Privacy Policy for MatchstickSimulator

Summary & Zero-Data Guarantee: MatchstickSimulator is a lightweight, offline casual simulation app created strictly for entertainment and sensory relaxation. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device telemetry, identifiers, or location data whatsoever.

1. Purpose & Entertainment Simulation Disclaimer

MatchstickSimulator is designed exclusively for tactile satisfaction, stress relief, cozy virtual bonfire vibes, acoustic relaxation, and digital entertainment. The application recreates the visual appearance of wooden matchsticks, an interactive friction striking strip, dynamic flame bezier flickering, progressive wood charring physics, rising smoke particle effects, and soothing ASMR fire crackling acoustics.

The application is a pure software simulation and does not produce real-world heat, flame, smoke, combustion, chemical odors, or fire hazards. It is 100% safe for users of all ages.

2. Zero Personal Information Collection

We respect your privacy unconditionally. MatchstickSimulator operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge and Zero-Data Collection policy:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for, gather, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or social media credentials.
  • No User Account: The application does not require account creation, login, cloud authentication, or personal profile registration.
  • No Financial Data: There are no in-app purchases, micro-transactions, paid subscriptions, or banking data transactions processed within the application.

3. Zero Runtime & Sensitive Device Permissions

MatchstickSimulator requires ZERO runtime or sensitive permissions from your Android operating system. Specifically:

  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The app never captures audio recordings, photographs, or visual media from your device.
  • No Location Tracking: The app has zero access to GPS, fine or coarse location sensors, or network-derived position.
  • No Storage Access: The application does not read or inspect your photos, media libraries, documents, external storage, or filesystem.
  • No Contacts, SMS, or Phone State: The app never accesses your address book, call logs, phone state, or messaging history.

4. Complete Offline Architecture & No Analytics Tracking

The entire application runs entirely self-contained on your mobile device:

  • 100% Offline Capability: All visual rendering, flame physics, wood charring algorithms, and acoustic sound synthesis are processed locally on your device without requiring internet connectivity or mobile network access.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not integrate tracking SDKs, marketing beacons, telemetry libraries, or behavioural monitoring software (such as Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Adjust).
  • No Advertisements: MatchstickSimulator contains zero ad banners, interstitial popups, rewarded video ads, or third-party advertising networks.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

Because MatchstickSimulator does not collect, retain, or disclose any personal data from any user, it is completely compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and worldwide child safety frameworks. The application is safe, friendly, and non-hazardous for children, families, and users of all ages.
