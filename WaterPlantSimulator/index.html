Privacy Policy for Water Plant Simulator

Application Package: com.datafaiq.waterplantsimulator • Effective Date: October 14, 2026
🛡️
Privacy Commitment Summary: Water Plant Simulator is an offline casual entertainment and educational simulation utility. It does NOT collect, store, log, transmit, or share any personal data, location records, or device identifiers. It requires zero sensitive device permissions and contains zero tracking mechanisms.

1. Purpose and Scope of Application

Water Plant Simulator is designed and distributed exclusively as an offline educational and casual entertainment simulation. It allows users to visualize, operate, and experience an interactive model of an industrial water purification and filtration plant, including pump controls, multi-media sediment filters, Reverse Osmosis (RO) membranes, UV sterilization tubes, and tank level gauges. Your privacy is paramount to us, and this document provides transparent disclosure regarding our practices.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Water Plant Simulator strictly collects NO personal or non-personal data from its users:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request, access, collect, or store your name, email address, physical location, telephone number, user accounts, or contacts.
  • No Hardware or Network Identifiers: We do not collect device IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or IP addresses.
  • No Geographic Location Access: The application does not request or track precise GPS coordinates or coarse cellular/Wi-Fi location data.
  • No Media, Camera, or File Access: The app has zero access to your photos, media library, internal storage, microphone, or camera.

3. Zero Runtime Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

The application is engineered as a self-contained, offline Android application:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: No runtime permissions are requested or declared in the Android manifest. The app operates safely within the standard Android application sandbox without needing elevated system access.
  • 100% Offline Operation: All procedural fluid animations, sound synthesizer audio assets, particle dynamics, and graphic illustrations are bundled directly within the application package (.apk/.aab). An active internet connection or cellular data connection is not required to operate the simulator.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Telemetry

Water Plant Simulator incorporates NO third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or remote telemetry frameworks. We do not partner with data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics providers. No information is transmitted to external servers or cloud repositories.

5. Children's Online Privacy & Family Safety (COPPA Compliance)

Water Plant Simulator complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements. Because our app contains zero advertisements, collects zero personal information, requires zero device permissions, and features harmless visual simulations suitable for all age groups, it is completely safe and appropriate for children and families.

6. Security and Sandboxing

Because the app functions strictly offline and does not transmit data over the internet, your device is fully protected from data eavesdropping, man-in-the-middle attacks, and remote data breaches. All settings and preferences are stored locally in private application sandbox storage and are completely erased if the user uninstalls the application.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain ongoing alignment with Google Play Developer Program policies and relevant data protection laws. Any updates will be published directly to this public URL with an updated effective date.
