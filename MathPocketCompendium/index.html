Privacy Policy

MathPocket Compendium (com.articode.app.mathpocketcompendium)
100% Offline • Complete Handbook • Zero Permissions

1. Zero Data Collection

MathPocket Compendium collects zero personal data, search queries, bookmarked equations, telemetry metrics, crash reports, or device identifiers. Everything you study remains strictly private on your phone.

2. Zero Permissions

MathPocket Compendium requests zero dangerous or runtime Android permissions:

  • No access to Storage, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or Location.
  • No network tracking, background syncing, ad networks, or behavioral trackers.

3. 100% Client-Side Offline Utility

Every Algebra, Geometry, Calculus, and Probability theorem, symbol legend, numerical constant, and worked derivation operates offline directly from local bundled app assets. Bookmarks and Light/Sepia/Dark preferences stay locally in Android SharedPreferences and wipe clean upon uninstall.

4. Age Rating & Policy

MathPocket Compendium is intended for students, educators, and general STEM audiences (13+ / 18+), collects zero data regardless of user age, and satisfies COPPA and Google Play policies cleanly.
