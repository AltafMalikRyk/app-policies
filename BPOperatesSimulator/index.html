⚠️ Critical Non-Medical Entertainment Disclaimer

BPOperatesSimulator is strictly and exclusively an entertainment and roleplay simulation. This application CANNOT and DOES NOT measure real blood pressure, heart rate, pulse, or human physiological health parameters. Smartphones do not have medical pressure bladders, arterial transducers, or sphygmomanometer sensors. Never use this simulation for medical evaluations, health monitoring, diagnosis, or clinical decisions. Always consult licensed healthcare practitioners and clinical medical equipment for genuine medical care.

Privacy Summary: BPOperatesSimulator is engineered with an absolute zero-data architecture. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, health data, identifiers, or location data. It requires ZERO sensitive device permissions.

1. Purpose and Entertainment Scope

BPOperatesSimulator is designed purely as an interactive mechanical simulation of a classic manual blood pressure cuff (sphygmomanometer) and aneroid dial gauge. Users can tap the virtual rubber bulb to simulate inflation, open the rotary air valve to trigger air hissing sounds, watch needle deflection physics, and listen to simulated Korotkoff sounds. The software is built solely for casual roleplay, fun interactivity, stress relief, and educational illustration of manual medical instruments.

2. Zero Personal and Health Data Collection

BPOperatesSimulator does not collect, record, process, store, transmit, or monetize any personal or health information whatsoever:

  • No Health or Biometric Data: The application does not collect, measure, or access heart rate, pulse, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, body temperature, or medical history.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request, access, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone numbers, contact books, or user accounts.
  • No Unique Device Identifiers: We do not gather hardware MAC addresses, IMEI codes, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or device serial numbers.
  • No Precise or Coarse Location: The application does not request GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular network geographical location data.
  • No Storage or Media Access: The application does not access internal device storage, external SD cards, photos, videos, or camera sensors.

3. Zero Runtime Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

BPOperatesSimulator is engineered as a completely self-contained, standalone offline application:

  • Zero Runtime Permissions: No dangerous or sensitive Android permissions are declared in the application manifest or requested at runtime (No Camera, Microphone, Body Sensors, Storage, Location, or Contacts).
  • 100% Offline Execution: All procedural dial physics, acoustic waveforms, and user interface components are bundled directly inside the APK/AAB package. No network connectivity or active data plan is required to use the simulator.
