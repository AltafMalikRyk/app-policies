Official Privacy Policy

Exhaust Fan Simulator

Package: com.datafaiq.exhaustfansimulator Last Updated: October 2026 Platform: Android Only

Privacy Commitment: Exhaust Fan Simulator is an offline casual utility and acoustic entertainment application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data or telemetry whatsoever.

1. Overview & Purpose

Exhaust Fan Simulator is developed purely for casual entertainment, study concentration, sleep assistance, and relaxation. It simulates the mechanical rotation, visual airflow vortex, shutter louvers, and soothing acoustic hum of a ventilation exhaust fan. The application is entirely self-contained and functions 100% offline.

2. Information Collection & Zero Permissions

Our philosophy is rooted in complete privacy by design:

Zero Personal Data

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device hardware identifiers.

Zero Sensitive Permissions

The app requests NO camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location access. It operates without any runtime permissions.

100% Offline Architecture

The app operates completely without an internet connection. No network requests or analytics pings are ever initiated.

3. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

Exhaust Fan Simulator contains:

  • No advertising SDKs: No banners, interstitials, or reward video ad networks.
  • No tracking or analytics frameworks: No user profiling, behavioural heatmaps, or third-party cookies.
  • No external telemetry: No data is collected or sent to remote servers.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Exhaust Fan Simulator is suitable for users of all ages, including children and families. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we are in full compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and global child safety standards.

5. Device Safety & Battery Efficiency

The simulation uses optimized Canvas rendering and native Android audio playback to ensure efficient hardware utilization. The built-in sleep timer allows the fan to automatically shut off after your chosen duration to prevent unnecessary battery drain during sleep.
