Privacy Policy - StaplerSimulator

Effective Date: October 2026 | Application: StaplerSimulator (com.datafaiq.staplersimulator)

Quick Summary: StaplerSimulator is a 100% offline, standalone casual entertainment simulation app. It collects zero personal data, requests zero sensitive or runtime device permissions, uses no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and connects to no external servers.

1. Entertainment Simulation Disclaimer

StaplerSimulator is designed solely for tactile fidgeting, casual office roleplay, stress relief, and soothing mechanical ASMR audio simulation. All staplers, virtual paper sheets, silver staple pins, and clamping animations depicted within the application are purely digital entertainment representations and have no physical binding capacity or official utility outside the app.

2. Zero Data Collection Policy

We respect and prioritize your absolute privacy. When using StaplerSimulator:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not request, harvest, transmit, or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact books, or accounts.
  • No Location Tracking: The application has no access to GPS, Wi-Fi network positions, or cellular triangulations.
  • No File or Storage Inspection: We never inspect, read, or access your device photos, gallery, files, external storage, or documents.
  • No Keystroke or Sensory Logging: Your virtual staple positions exist solely in volatile device memory (RAM) during your session and are automatically cleared upon app termination or manual document wipe.

3. Zero Sensitive Device Permissions

StaplerSimulator is engineered to be fully self-contained. The application explicitly does not request nor utilize any sensitive Android runtime permissions:

  • android.permission.CAMERA - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE - NOT REQUESTED
  • android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE - NOT REQUESTED

Non-sensitive device hardware APIs such as standard vibration (haptic recoil feedback) and low-latency audio sonification are used strictly locally to deliver satisfying physical feedback when the stapler lever strikes the anvil plate.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Behavioral Tracking

StaplerSimulator contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for monetization (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Meta Audience Network) and no user behavioral analytics (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer). The app operates without background telemetry or diagnostic profiling.

5. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Because StaplerSimulator does not collect, retain, or transmit personal information from any user, it is completely compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as well as global data privacy regulations (GDPR). The application is safe and appropriate for users of all ages, including children under 13.

6. Data Security and Transmission

Because no network requests are dispatched by the app, there is no risk of intercepting or leaking personal data in transit or on remote servers. All interactive app states remain strictly localized to the device hardware.
