Privacy Policy

FuelCost Tool (com.articode.app.fuelcosttool)
100% Offline • Zero Permissions • Zero Tracking

1. Zero Data Collection

FuelCost Tool collects zero personal information, trip history, analytics, advertising identifiers, or diagnostic logs. The application does not maintain user accounts and transmits nothing off your device.

2. Zero Permissions Architecture

FuelCost Tool requests zero dangerous or runtime Android permissions:

  • No GPS, coarse location, or background trip tracking.
  • No access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Storage.
  • No advertising SDKs or hidden network trackers.

3. Fully Offline Processing

All fuel consumption, metric/imperial conversions, and passenger cost splits happen completely offline in volatile local device memory. Theme, haptic, and decimal precision preferences are stored strictly locally in SharedPreferences and deleted when the app is uninstalled.

4. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

FuelCost Tool collects zero data from any user regardless of age and fully complies with COPPA and international child privacy protection regulations.
