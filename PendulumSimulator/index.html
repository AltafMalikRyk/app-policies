Privacy Policy for Pendulum Simulator

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026
Privacy Summary: Pendulum Simulator is an offline casual entertainment and harmonic physics simulation app. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user identifiers, or location data. It requires ZERO sensitive device permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access).

1. Purpose and Scope

Pendulum Simulator is designed and distributed exclusively for casual entertainment, harmonic relaxation, rhythm cadence, and interactive physics simulation. The application offers interactive pendulum bob controls, realistic harmonic motion physics, celestial gravity simulations (Earth, Moon, Zero-G), aesthetic motion trails, and soothing rhythmic tick acoustics. We are committed to complete transparency and user privacy.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Pendulum Simulator does not collect, track, log, process, transmit, or store any personal information whatsoever.

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or accounts.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, IP addresses, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: The application does not access GPS or network-based location services.
  • No Files or Media Access: The app does not read, write, or access your camera, microphone, internal files, or external storage.

3. Device Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

Pendulum Simulator is built from the ground up as a fully self-contained offline application with zero runtime permissions:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: No dangerous or sensitive Android permissions are requested in the manifest or during runtime.
  • Self-Contained Local Operation: All physical simulation engines, motion shaders, vector mathematics, and audio assets reside locally within the application package. No internet connectivity is required.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

Pendulum Simulator contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, profiling trackers, or telemetry analytics tools. We do not display banner or video ads, nor do we partner with commercial data brokers or analytics networks.

5. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

Pendulum Simulator complies strictly with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the app displays zero ads, collects zero personal information, requires no device permissions, and offers a harmless, educational physics visualization, it is safe and suitable for users of all ages, including children and families.

6. Security and Data Protection

Because Pendulum Simulator functions strictly locally without communicating with remote servers or cloud databases, there is zero risk of remote data breaches or unauthorized access. The app executes securely within Android's protected application sandbox.

7. Policy Modifications

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to ensure continuing alignment with legal requirements and Google Play policies. Any updates will be published directly to this page with an updated revision date.
