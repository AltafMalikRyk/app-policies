Privacy Policy

Gumball Dispenser Simulator
Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

This Privacy Policy describes how Gumball Dispenser Simulator (the "Application") respects and protects your privacy. We believe in total user trust and guarantee complete privacy protections for everyone who uses our simulation.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Gumball Dispenser Simulator does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or disclose any personal data, identifiers, or usage telemetry whatsoever. The application functions 100% locally and completely offline.

2. Personal Information Collection

The Application does not require registration, account setup, or user login. We collect zero personal details:

  • No names, usernames, or nicknames.
  • No physical addresses, GPS locations, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.
  • No email addresses, phone numbers, or social media handles.
  • No financial, billing, or transaction details.

3. Device & Technical Telemetry

We do not gather or process technical telemetry from your mobile device:

  • Zero advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • Zero persistent hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, serial number).
  • Zero diagnostic logs, crash reports, or operational analytics transmitted over network.

4. Application Permissions & Usage

Gumball Dispenser Simulator requests minimal Android system privileges to deliver physical simulation features:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly to operate the device vibrator motor, replicating mechanical gear ratchets when turning the crank handle. This permission cannot access files, location, or private data.

The Application requests zero sensitive runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location access).

5. Offline Execution & Third-Party SDKs

The Application is built as a self-contained local experience:

  • No third-party analytics libraries (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Adjust).
  • No third-party advertising SDKs or ad mediation networks.
  • No third-party social plugins or trackers.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Gumball Dispenser Simulator is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child data protection frameworks. Because we collect zero data from any user, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no user data is collected or transmitted to external servers, we hold no user data to retain or delete. All preferences (such as collected candy counts and sound preferences) are maintained locally on the client device and are purged instantly when the app is uninstalled or cleared via Android system settings.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or platform rules. Any updates will be published immediately to this URL with a revised effective date.
