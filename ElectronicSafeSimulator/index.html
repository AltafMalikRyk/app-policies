Privacy Policy

Electronic Safe Simulator • com.datafaiq.electronicsafesimulator

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines the privacy practices of the development team for Electronic Safe Simulator ("we," "our," or "the application"). We respect your fundamental right to privacy and are committed to maintaining a completely secure, transparent, and offline-first mobile environment.

Electronic Safe Simulator is designed as an interactive offline mechanical simulation app for entertainment, sound novelty, combination puzzle games, and tactile ASMR exploration. The application operates 100% locally on your device without collecting, transmitting, or sharing your personal information across external networks.

2. Information Collection and Use (Zero Personal Data)

We collect zero personally identifiable information (PII).

Electronic Safe Simulator does NOT collect, store, monitor, sell, or transmit any user information whatsoever. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Audio recordings, microphone data, or ambient sound inputs
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details
  • Precise or coarse geographic location data (GPS, IP, Wi-Fi)
  • Unique device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Advertising IDs)
  • Financial, banking, or actual payment transaction details
  • Behavioral telemetry, usage monitoring, or cloud analytics

User preferences configured within the tool (such as selected vault finish, user PIN combination, or sound volume) are maintained temporarily in device memory solely to preserve your session settings and are never transmitted outside your hardware.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Electronic Safe Simulator requires zero dangerous runtime permissions. The only hardware interaction requested is:

  • VIBRATE Permission: Used exclusively to trigger the device's built-in haptic vibrator motor when pressing digital keypad buttons, rotating the mechanical vault wheel, and retracting heavy locking bolts. This delivers realistic tactile mechanical feedback and can be toggled off at any time in the app settings.
  • Audio System: The app outputs built-in procedural sound effects via the standard Android SoundPool API. It does not record audio or access your microphone.

The app does NOT request camera, contacts, SMS, phone state, GPS location, or external storage access.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Electronic Safe Simulator contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or external telemetry systems. Because no third-party code is included, no third party has access to any information regarding your device or usage.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Electronic Safe Simulator is suitable for general audiences of all ages and adheres strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as well as global child privacy standards.

Because the app does not collect, track, or share any personal information whatsoever, children and families can safely enjoy the interactive vault puzzle simulation without any risk of personal data capture or transmission.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no personal data retained to delete. If you uninstall the application, all locally saved preferences and application cache files are completely and permanently removed by your operating system.

7. Security Practices

We prioritize security by implementing an offline-first, client-only architecture. Because no network connections are initiated and no user data is collected, the application is inherently safeguarded against remote data leaks and interception vulnerabilities.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory modifications. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of the application signifies your acceptance of any revisions.
