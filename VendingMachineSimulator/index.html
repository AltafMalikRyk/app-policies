Privacy Policy

Privacy Commitment: Vending Machine Simulator is a 100% offline casual arcade simulation app. We strictly do NOT collect, store, transmit, monetize, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or location information. Zero runtime permissions are required.

1 Entertainment Simulation Disclaimer

Vending Machine Simulator is created and distributed strictly for entertainment, casual gaming, tactile simulation, and nostalgic fun. All snacks, beverages, coin slots, credits, keypads, and mechanical dispensing animations featured in the application are entirely virtual simulations. No physical goods, actual food products, or real refreshments are sold or delivered.

2 Zero Payment & Financial Tracking

The application simulates the experience of inserting coins and dispensing treats using free, unlimited virtual tokens:

  • No Real-Money Transactions: The app does not include any real-money payment gateways, in-app purchases (IAP), credit card processing, digital wallets, or subscription plans.
  • No Financial Data Collection: We never ask for, access, process, or store bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, billing addresses, or financial records.

3 Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe in absolute digital privacy. Vending Machine Simulator does not collect, record, process, or transmit any user data:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, usernames, or physical locations.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not log IMEI numbers, hardware serials, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or IP addresses.
  • No User Account or Registration: You can launch and enjoy the application instantly without creating accounts, logging in, or providing social profiles.

4 Zero Runtime Permissions & Offline Architecture

Vending Machine Simulator is engineered to run as an isolated, self-contained offline application:

  • Zero Runtime Permissions: The application does not request sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your Camera, Microphone, Storage/Photos, Location, Contacts, Calendar, or Phone State.
  • 100% Offline Functionality: All graphics, sound effects, vector illustrations, and physics animations are packaged within the application. No internet connection or mobile data plan is necessary to run the simulator.

5 No Third-Party Tracking & No Advertising

Vending Machine Simulator does NOT incorporate any third-party behavioral trackers, telemetry frameworks, or advertising networks:

  • No banner ads, interstitial popups, rewarded video ads, or native advertisements.
  • No analytics SDKs tracking user behavior, session times, or click maps.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute user information to data brokers or marketing firms.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Vending Machine Simulator is family-friendly and fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as well as the Google Play Families Policy. Because the application does not gather any personal data, contains no advertising, has no social features, and contains no external data collection endpoints, it provides a safe, welcoming, and worry-free entertainment environment for children, students, and families.

7 Security & Data Safety

Because Vending Machine Simulator does not transmit data over the internet or communicate with external cloud servers, there is no risk of remote data breaches, intercepted transmissions, or unauthorized server exposure. The app executes securely within Android's sandboxed runtime environment.

8 Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to ensure continued compliance with evolving Google Play Store developer guidelines or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date.
