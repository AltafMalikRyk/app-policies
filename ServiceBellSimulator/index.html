Privacy Policy

Service Bell Simulator
Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

This Privacy Policy outlines how Service Bell Simulator (the "Application") handles user privacy. We believe in absolute transparency and state unconditionally that we value your privacy above all else.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Service Bell Simulator does not collect, record, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data, identifiers, or usage telemetry whatsoever. The application functions 100% locally and completely offline.

2. Personal Information Collection

The Application does not require registration, account setup, or login credentials. We collect zero personal details including:

  • No names, usernames, or contact information.
  • No physical addresses, GPS locations, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.
  • No email addresses, phone numbers, or social media accounts.
  • No financial, billing, or transaction details.

3. Device & Technical Data

We do not capture or process technical telemetry. Specifically:

  • Zero advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • Zero persistent hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, serial number).
  • Zero diagnostic logs, crash dumps, or operational analytics sent over network.

4. Application Permissions & Usage

Service Bell Simulator requires minimal Android system privileges to deliver physical simulation features:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly to drive the device's internal haptic vibrator motor when the user strikes the bell plunger. This permission cannot read data, access contacts, or track locations.

The Application requests zero sensitive runtime permissions (no camera, no microphone, no storage read/write, no location).

5. Offline Execution & Third-Party SDKs

The Application runs strictly offline on your device:

  • No third-party analytics libraries (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Adjust).
  • No third-party advertising SDKs or ad mediation networks.
  • No third-party social plugins or trackers.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Service Bell Simulator is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child data protection frameworks. Because we collect zero data from any user, we do not knowingly or unknowingly harvest personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no user data is collected or transmitted to external servers, we hold no user data to retain or delete. All preferences (such as selected bell metal, volume, and tally counter numbers) are maintained locally on the client device and are purged instantly when the app is uninstalled or cleared via Android system settings.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or platform rules. Any updates will be published immediately to this URL with a revised effective date.
