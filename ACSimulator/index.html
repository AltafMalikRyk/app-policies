Privacy Policy for AC Simulator

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview and Purpose

AC Simulator is designed and distributed strictly as a lightweight, offline simulation and ambient sound utility for relaxation, study focus, sleep sound assistance, and casual entertainment. The application provides simulated visual air-vent louvers, cooling airflow animations, and soothing compressor and fan white noise acoustics. Your privacy is fundamental to our core values, and this privacy policy comprehensively details our strict data privacy standards.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

AC Simulator does not collect, track, record, transmit, store, or share any personal information or sensitive user data whatsoever.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, access, or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or home address.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect or monitor IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, IP addresses, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Location Tracking: The application does not collect, access, or determine precise (GPS) or approximate network-based location data.
  • No Files or Media Access: AC Simulator does not read, write, or access any files, photos, videos, or external media on your device.

3. Device Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

AC Simulator is built from the ground up as a fully self-contained offline application with zero runtime permissions:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: The application does not declare or request sensitive Android permissions, including Camera, Microphone, Read/Write Storage, Contacts, Location, or Phone State.
  • Self-Contained Local Operation: All graphical assets, procedural air breeze animations, and soothing acoustic audio loops reside locally within the installed application package. No active internet or data connection is required to enjoy all features of the simulation.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

AC Simulator incorporates NO third-party advertising networks, telemetry tools, profiling trackers, or analytics SDKs. There are no background analytic trackers monitoring your app interactions. We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute user data to any commercial data brokers, advertising partners, or external entities.

5. Children's Privacy & Google Play Family Policy Compliance

AC Simulator strictly complies with Google Play Families Policy Requirements and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the application is completely non-intrusive, displays zero advertisements, collects zero personal data, and requires no device permissions, it is completely safe and suitable for audiences of all ages, including young children and families.

6. Security and Data Protection

Because AC Simulator operates strictly on-device without data collection or remote server connectivity, there are no cloud databases, remote servers, or transmission pipelines where your information could be exposed or breached. The application runs within the standard secure Android application sandbox framework.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to ensure continuing alignment with legal requirements and Google Play policies. Any updates will be published directly to this page with an updated revision date.
