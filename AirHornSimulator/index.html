ℹ️ 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Air Horn Simulator. This application is an interactive audio-visual soundboard designed purely for sports celebrations, party noise making, DJ fanfare appreciation, and harmless novelty amusement.

We respect your privacy unconditionally. Air Horn Simulator operates completely offline on your device and does not monitor, track, store, or transmit any user data or device telemetry.

🛡️ 2. Information We Collect

Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, process, harvest, store, or transmit any personal information whatsoever. Specifically, we do not access:

  • User identity, real name, email, telephone number, or postal address.
  • Device serial numbers, IMEI, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware parameters.
  • Geographic locations, GPS coordinates, or approximate network geolocation.
  • Camera imagery, microphone recordings, photo galleries, or local storage documents.
  • Contacts, social media links, calendar events, or payment credentials.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Information

Because zero personal information is gathered, no user data is ever processed, analyzed, commercialized, or shared. All user preferences—such as selected canister themes, blast modes, and volume settings—are maintained purely in transient local memory during runtime.

📱 4. Device Permissions

Air Horn Simulator requires no sensitive or invasive Android runtime permissions. The only permission declared is:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to generate tactile vibration pulses during horn blasts. This permission cannot read private user files or connect to network services.

🚫 5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Air Horn Simulator contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, telemetry libraries, or data broker links. There are zero integrations with platforms such as AdMob, Unity, Firebase Telemetry, or Meta Audience Network.

👶 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Air Horn Simulator is safe, fun, and suitable for users of all age brackets, including children under 13. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K regulations, and Google Play's Families Policy. No personal data is knowingly requested from any child.

🔒 7. Data Security & Storage

Because the app contains no remote server sockets, cloud databases, or network communication, your mobile device remains fully isolated and secure while using the simulation.
