Privacy Policy

Jack In The Box Simulator
Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jack In The Box Simulator (the "Application") protects user privacy. We are committed to complete transparency and guarantee absolute protection of user privacy.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Jack In The Box Simulator does not collect, record, transmit, monetize, or disclose any personal data, identifiers, or technical telemetry. The application functions 100% locally and completely offline.

2. Personal Information Collection

The Application requires no user accounts, logins, or profile creation. We collect zero personal information:

  • No names, usernames, or nicknames.
  • No physical addresses, GPS locations, or Wi-Fi positioning data.
  • No email addresses, telephone numbers, or social media handles.
  • No payment, credit card, or banking details.

3. Device & Technical Telemetry

We do not gather or process technical telemetry from your mobile device:

  • Zero advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • Zero persistent hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, serial number).
  • Zero crash logs, analytics events, or diagnostic traces sent to remote servers.

4. Application Permissions & Usage

Jack In The Box Simulator requests minimal Android system privileges necessary solely for its physical simulation mechanics:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly to operate the device vibrator motor, delivering tactile ratchet clicks when winding the crank and heavy recoil vibration when Jack springs open. This permission cannot access files, location, or private data.

The Application requests zero sensitive runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location access).

5. Offline Operation & Third-Party SDKs

The Application is built as a self-contained local experience:

  • Contains no third-party analytics libraries (e.g. Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel).
  • Contains no third-party advertising frameworks or ad mediation networks.
  • Contains no third-party cloud trackers or remote profiling SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Jack In The Box Simulator strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. Because zero personal data is ever collected, the Application is completely safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no data is ever transmitted to external servers, we hold no user data to store, retain, or purge. User preferences (such as selected box theme and sound settings) remain entirely local on the device and are deleted permanently upon uninstalling the application.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to maintain compliance with relevant app store policies and legal regulations. Any revisions will be published immediately to this web page with an updated effective date.
