BubbleWrapSimulator Privacy Policy

App: BubbleWrapSimulator Package: com.datafaiq.bubblewrapsimulator Effective Date: October 2026

Our Core Privacy Commitment

BubbleWrapSimulator is a 100% standalone, offline sensory simulation and entertainment app. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or usage telemetry. No account is required, and zero runtime device permissions are requested.

1 Entertainment & Sensory Utility Disclaimer

BubbleWrapSimulator is designed strictly for casual entertainment, sensory decompression, stress relief, and tactile ASMR fidgeting. It emulates the interactive mechanics, crisp acoustics, and physical response of bursting packaging bubble wrap sheets. The app is intended purely for leisure, amusement, and sensory satisfaction.

2 Zero Personal Information Collection

We respect your privacy unconditionally. BubbleWrapSimulator does not gather any identifiable or sensitive personal information whatsoever. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • Device hardware IDs, advertising IDs, MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
  • Biometric data, sensor recordings, or touch pressure telemetry.
  • User keystrokes, clipboard content, or installed application lists.
  • Location data (neither GPS coarse nor fine location is accessible).

3 Zero Sensitive or Runtime Device Permissions

The application is designed to be completely self-contained. The Android Manifest requires ZERO sensitive or runtime permissions.

No Camera or Microphone

No video or audio input is ever accessed or recorded.

No Storage Access

No access to your personal files, gallery, photos, or media.

No Location Services

Zero tracking of geographic coordinates or network localization.

No Contacts or Accounts

Zero access to personal address books, social accounts, or phone identity.

4 Tactile Haptic Vibration & Audio Playback

To provide realistic sensory satisfaction, the application utilizes standard Android audio and haptic feedback capabilities:

  • Sound Effects: Crisp bubble rupture audio files are packaged directly within the application and played locally via native low-latency system audio APIs. No audio is ever captured, analyzed, or uploaded.
  • Tactile Haptics: Gentle vibration pulses accompany each popped bubble to simulate physical plastic resistance. Haptic vibration is processed locally on the device and can be customized or fully disabled in the app settings at any time.

5 No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising SDKs

BubbleWrapSimulator does not embed any third-party analytics SDKs, behavioral telemetry tools, marketing trackers, or third-party ad networks. There are no background data transmissions, invisible pixels, or user profiling mechanisms.

6 Child Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because BubbleWrapSimulator does not collect any personal data from any user regardless of age, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Family Policy requirements. The app is completely safe, benign, and suitable for users of all ages, including children and families.

7 Local Application Preferences

Any settings you adjust—such as pop sound volume, vibration intensity level, and bubble wrap color theme (Classic Clear, Neon Pink, Ocean Blue, Emerald Mint, Sunset Amber)—are saved strictly locally on your device hardware. This data never leaves your device and is wiped immediately if you uninstall the application or clear app storage.
