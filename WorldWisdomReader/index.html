Privacy Policy

WorldWisdom Reader (com.articode.app.worldwisdomreader)
100% Offline • Complete Handbook • Zero Permissions

1. Zero Data Collection

WorldWisdom Reader collects zero personal data, search queries, saved bookmarks, analytics telemetry, crash logs, or device identifiers. Everything you read and save stays strictly private on your phone.

2. Zero Permissions

WorldWisdom Reader requests zero dangerous or runtime Android permissions:

  • No access to Storage, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or Location.
  • No internet requests, background syncing, ad networks, or behavioral trackers.

3. 100% Client-Side Offline Utility

Every proverb, native script passage, phonetic transliteration, English translation, and cultural context note runs offline directly from local app assets. Bookmarks and Light/Sepia/Dark preferences stay locally in Android SharedPreferences and wipe clean on uninstall.

4. Age Rating & Policy

WorldWisdom Reader is designed for general literature and humanities audiences (13+ / 18+), collects zero data regardless of age, and meets COPPA and Google Play privacy policies cleanly.
