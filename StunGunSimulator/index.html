Privacy Policy for Stun Gun Simulator

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 | Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Stun Gun Simulator ("the Application"), developed by independent mobile developers as a self-contained, offline novelty soundboard and interactive sensory simulation. The application is built solely for casual amusement, novelty pranks, roleplay soundscapes, and sensory entertainment.

Safety Declaration: Stun Gun Simulator is 100% harmless. It does not deliver electric voltage or physical current, nor does it generate hazardous electrical energy. It is an artistic audio-visual simulation.

We are fully committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent privacy practices and confirms our strict adherence to zero personal data gathering.

2. Information We Collect

Stun Gun Simulator does NOT collect, record, process, transmit, or store any personal information.

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, accounts, or physical addresses.
  • No Unique Device Identifiers: We do not read, harvest, or track device identifiers such as IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: The application has no location-sensing capabilities; neither fine nor coarse GPS/network location is accessed.
  • No Media or File Access: The application does not read, modify, or upload photos, documents, media files, or internal storage databases.

3. How We Use Information (Offline Execution)

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no user information to process, distribute, analyze, or monetize:

  • 100% Offline-First Architecture: All simulation components—visual rendering engines, electrical arc synthesis, audio assets, and tactile vibration triggers—are packaged locally within the APK/AAB bundle.
  • Zero Telemetry & Analytics: The app contains zero event logging, behavioral metrics, screen tracking, or telemetry pings.
  • Zero Server Communication: The app never initiates outbound network socket connections or sends HTTP requests to any remote cloud server.

4. Device Permissions

Stun Gun Simulator does not request any sensitive runtime permissions. It uses only standard non-runtime hardware utility declarations:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Normal protection permission utilized exclusively to drive the device's tactile haptic motor to simulate physical stun recoil when the firing trigger is pressed.
  • FLASHLIGHT (android.permission.FLASHLIGHT): Optional utility feature used to pulse the rear camera LED flash in tandem with the visual electric arc effect when enabled in settings. No camera images, video feeds, or visual data are ever captured or accessed.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Stun Gun Simulator contains absolutely NO third-party advertising SDKs, commercial trackers, profiling services, or data brokerage tools. You will encounter zero banner ads, zero interstitial video popups, and zero third-party telemetry libraries. We never sell, lease, or share any user details.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Stun Gun Simulator is completely compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy Requirements. Because our app contains zero tracking, zero personal data harvesting, and zero inappropriate content, it is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data Security & Storage

The application operates entirely in-memory during active sessions. User preferences (such as audio volume, selected skin, and haptic level) reside exclusively in ephemeral local app memory. No sensitive databases or unencrypted configuration files exist. The app runs securely within standard Android sandbox boundaries.

8. Policy Changes & Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future Android operating system compatibility or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published directly to this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updated terms.
