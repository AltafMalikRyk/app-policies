Summary & Zero-Data Guarantee: DoorbellSimulator is a lightweight, offline casual simulation utility created strictly for entertainment, sound novelty, and pet training cues. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device telemetry, identifiers, or location data whatsoever.

1. Purpose & Entertainment Simulation Disclaimer

DoorbellSimulator is designed exclusively for tactile satisfaction, acoustic entertainment, friendly pranks, pet desensitization cues, and casual digital soundboard play. The application recreates the visual appearance of a modern architectural smart doorbell unit with a responsive mechanical push button, illuminated glowing LED halo ring, and studio-quality synthesized acoustic chimes (Classic Ding-Dong, Westminster Chimes, Cyber Intercom, and Vintage Brass Bell).

The application is a pure software simulation and does not physically connect to real-world smart doorbells, home security systems, electrical wiring, or external hardware. It is 100% safe for users of all ages.

2. Zero Personal Information Collection

We respect your privacy unconditionally. DoorbellSimulator operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge and Zero-Data Collection policy:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for, gather, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or social media credentials.
  • No User Account: The application does not require account creation, login, cloud authentication, or personal profile registration.
  • No Financial Data: There are no in-app purchases, micro-transactions, paid subscriptions, or banking data transactions processed within the application.

3. Zero Runtime & Sensitive Device Permissions

DoorbellSimulator requires ZERO runtime or sensitive permissions from your Android operating system. Specifically:

  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The app never captures audio recordings, photographs, or visual media from your device.
  • No Location Tracking: The app has zero access to GPS, fine or coarse location sensors, or network-derived position.
  • No Storage Access: The application does not read or inspect your photos, media libraries, documents, external storage, or filesystem.
  • No Contacts, SMS, or Phone State: The app never accesses your address book, call logs, phone state, or messaging history.

4. Complete Offline Architecture & No Analytics Tracking

The entire application runs entirely self-contained on your mobile device:

  • 100% Offline Capability: All visual rendering, button press animations, and chime audio synthesis are processed locally on your device without requiring internet connectivity or mobile network access.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not integrate tracking SDKs, marketing beacons, telemetry libraries, or behavioural monitoring software (such as Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Adjust).
  • No Advertisements: DoorbellSimulator contains zero ad banners, interstitial popups, rewarded video ads, or third-party advertising networks.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

Because DoorbellSimulator does not collect, retain, or disclose any personal data from any user, it is completely compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and worldwide child safety frameworks. The application is safe, friendly, and non-hazardous for children, families, and users of all ages.
