Privacy Policy

KalamClassic Reader (com.articode.app.kalamclassicreader)
100% Offline • Complete Handbook • Zero Permissions

1. Zero Data Collection

KalamClassic Reader collects zero personal data, search queries, reading habits, crash analytics, or device identifiers. We never collect, sell, or share any information about you or what poetry selections you read.

2. Zero Permissions

KalamClassic Reader requests zero dangerous or runtime Android permissions:

  • No access to Storage, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or Location.
  • No internet connection, background syncing, third-party ad networks, or behavioral telemetry.

3. 100% Client-Side Offline Utility

All classical poetry texts, translations, and literary notes are bundled locally inside the app package with zero external network dependency. Your saved bookmarks, font size, serif/sans choice, and Light/Sepia/Dark theme remain locally in private Android SharedPreferences and erase on uninstall.

4. Age Rating & Policy

KalamClassic Reader is designed for general literature audiences (13+ / 18+), collects zero data from any user regardless of age, and complies cleanly with COPPA and international privacy standards.
