Privacy Policy

Google Play Compliant Certified Family Safe Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Privacy at a Glance: Fish Aquarium Simulator is an offline casual entertainment and relaxation app. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, log, or sell any personal data, identifiers, or location information. It requires zero sensitive device permissions.

1. Purpose and Scope

Fish Aquarium Simulator is developed purely as an offline virtual entertainment, stress relief, and acoustic relaxation simulator. The application allows users to observe colorful swimming tropical fish, interact by dropping floating food flakes, enjoy expanding water ripples, customize ambient lighting, and listen to soothing underwater bubbling soundscapes. We are firmly committed to user privacy and digital safety.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Fish Aquarium Simulator does not collect, record, process, retain, or transmit any personal information whatsoever:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We never ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, physical address, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not capture or access hardware serial numbers, IMEI codes, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or network IP addresses.
  • No Location Data: The application has no location tracking features and does not access GPS or network-derived coordinates.
  • No Media or File Access: The application does not read or write to user photo libraries, documents, camera, microphone, or external storage.

3. Zero Runtime Permissions & 100% Offline Architecture

Fish Aquarium Simulator has been purposely engineered as a fully self-contained offline application:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: No runtime or sensitive Android permissions are declared in the manifest or requested during usage (No Camera, No Microphone, No Location, No Storage, No Contacts).
  • Completely Offline Operation: All fish simulation models, fluid animations, water ripples, caustics, and acoustic sound loops are stored directly within the application package. No internet connectivity or mobile cellular data is required to use the simulator.

4. No Third-Party Analytics, Tracking, or Advertising

Fish Aquarium Simulator contains NO third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or user profiling SDKs. We do not integrate tracking libraries or cloud telemetry tools. We will never sell, rent, monetize, or distribute user metrics to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA) & Google Play Families Policy

Fish Aquarium Simulator is designed to provide safe, wholesome casual entertainment for audiences of all ages, including children and families. Because the application collects zero data, contains zero advertising, requires zero device permissions, and features gentle marine simulation, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy requirements.

6. Security and Sandboxed Execution

Because Fish Aquarium Simulator runs completely offline within Android's protected application sandbox without communicating with any external servers, your device remains private and secure. There is zero risk of data interception, remote tampering, or external leakage.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to maintain conformity with evolving Google Play Store guidelines. The latest version will always remain publicly available at this URL with an updated effective date.
