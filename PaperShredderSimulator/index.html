1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to PaperShredderSimulator, an offline, interactive sensory simulation application engineered exclusively for desktop paper shredder simulation, ASMR soundscapes, and casual stress-relief entertainment. We prioritize your personal privacy and safety above all else.

This Privacy Policy explains the operational practices of PaperShredderSimulator. In summary: we collect, store, transmit, process, and share ZERO personal or non-personal information. The application operates entirely locally on your Android device.

2. Pure Entertainment & ASMR Disclaimer

PaperShredderSimulator is developed solely for entertainment, casual fidgeting, and acoustic sensory relaxation (ASMR). The virtual documents presented in the app (such as Confidential Memos, Tax Audit Bills, Meeting Doodle Sheets, and Parking Violations) are entirely fictional, satirical, and simulated templates.

Simulated Destruction: Tearing or shredding virtual documents in the app produces animated visual ribbons and synthetic audio effects. No real documents are destroyed, and no actual confidential files on your device are ever accessed or modified.

4. Zero Device Permissions Required

PaperShredderSimulator complies with the strictest Android permission guidelines. The app operates with ZERO runtime permissions:

  • NO Camera Permission: The app never opens or accesses your camera.
  • NO Microphone Permission: The app never listens to or records your voice. All tearing and shredding sounds are synthetic assets stored locally in the package.
  • NO Storage or Media Access: The app cannot view, modify, or read your device's photo gallery, document folders, or external storage.
  • NO Contacts or Account Access: The app has zero visibility into your personal address book or logged-in accounts.

5. Local On-Device State Only

Any configuration options you adjust in the app (such as mechanical motor volume, haptic vibration toggle, cutting speed slider, or lifetime shredded sheets count) are stored exclusively in the volatile memory and sandboxed local cache of your device. These preference values never leave your hardware and can be cleared at any moment by clearing the application cache.
