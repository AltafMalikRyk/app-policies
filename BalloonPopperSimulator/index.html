ℹ️ 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Balloon Popper Simulator. This application is designed purely as a virtual sensory entertainment tool and novelty simulation, providing users with dynamic air pump physics, latex balloon expansion visuals, popping mechanics, and realistic acoustic feedback.

We believe deeply in digital privacy. Balloon Popper Simulator is engineered from the ground up to operate 100% locally on your mobile device without collecting, transmitting, or sharing any personal data whatsoever.

🛡️ 2. Information We Collect

Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, process, harvest, store, or sell any personal information. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Your real name, age, physical address, phone number, or email address.
  • Device identifiers, IMEI, Android advertising ID (AAID), or MAC addresses.
  • Precise or coarse geographic location coordinates.
  • Camera imagery, microphone recordings, media files, or user documents.
  • Biometric data, browsing history, contacts, or calendar entries.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Information

Because no personal data is ever collected or accessed, no user information is used, evaluated, monetized, or shared with any internal or external entities. All simulation state—including selected balloon color palettes, vibration preferences, and inflation parameters—is kept ephemerally in volatile system memory during app execution and never uploaded anywhere.

📱 4. Device Permissions

Balloon Popper Simulator requests no sensitive or dangerous runtime permissions. The only permission requested is:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Utilized exclusively to drive the device vibration motor for realistic tactile feedback during air pumping and balloon bursting effects. This permission access is strictly local and cannot access private user data.

🚫 5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Balloon Popper Simulator does NOT incorporate any third-party tracking frameworks, behavioral analytics, telemetry tools, or advertising networks. There are zero external SDK integrations such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network, Firebase Analytics, or data brokers.

👶 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Balloon Popper Simulator is completely safe and appropriate for audiences of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or retain personal data from children.

🔒 7. Data Security & Ephemeral Storage

Because there is no remote server infrastructure, database, or network communication, your mobile device remains fully isolated and secure while enjoying the simulation. No user telemetry is retained across app termination beyond local user interface preferences.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future features or regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be posted immediately to this URL along with an updated effective revision date.
