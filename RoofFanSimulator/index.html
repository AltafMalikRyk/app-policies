Privacy Policy for Roof Fan Simulator

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview and Purpose

Roof Fan Simulator is developed as a lightweight, purely offline simulation and relaxation application. The application provides simulated visual fan motion and soothing ambient motor white noise designed for sleep aid, focus, and casual entertainment. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us, and this document details our stringent privacy commitments.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Roof Fan Simulator does not collect, record, transmit, or store any personal information.

  • No personal identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses are accessed or collected.
  • No device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID, MAC address) are gathered.
  • No precise or approximate location information is collected.
  • No keystrokes, contact lists, photos, or files are accessed.

3. Device Permissions & Offline Operation

The application functions completely locally on your Android device without requiring sensitive permissions:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Storage, Contacts, Location, or Phone state.
  • Self-Contained Offline Utility: The simulation logic, audio generation, and UI assets are fully embedded inside the application package. No network connection is required for simulation functionality.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Analytics

Roof Fan Simulator contains no third-party tracking software, advertising SDKs, profiling utilities, or external telemetry libraries. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing partners.

5. Children's Privacy & Google Play Family Policy

Roof Fan Simulator complies with Google Play Families Policy Requirements and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the app collects absolutely zero data and serves no targeted advertisements, it is completely safe for audiences of all ages, including children and families.

6. Security and Data Protection

Because our application operates entirely on-device and collects no user data, there are no databases, external servers, or cloud storage points holding your information. The application runs within standard Android sandboxed environments adhering to strict platform security guidelines.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of the current policy terms.
