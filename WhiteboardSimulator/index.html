Privacy Policy for Whiteboard Simulator

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026
Privacy Summary: Whiteboard Simulator is an offline casual entertainment and sketching simulation app. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user identifiers, or location data. Drawing state is strictly ephemeral in-memory, and the app requires ZERO device permissions (no photo library, camera, or storage access).

1. Purpose and Scope

Whiteboard Simulator is designed and distributed exclusively for casual entertainment, creative doodling, sensory drawing relaxation, and virtual marker sketching simulation. The app offers a virtual dry-erase whiteboard canvas with realistic marker strokes, customizable ink palettes, felt eraser simulations, and soothing marker squeak acoustics. We are deeply committed to user privacy and provide this policy to explain our strict zero-data principles.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Whiteboard Simulator does not collect, record, track, log, process, transmit, or disclose any personal user data whatsoever.

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or accounts.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, IP addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request or access precise GPS coordinates or network-derived coarse location.
  • No Media or File Access: The app does not access your photos, media gallery, camera, microphone, internal files, or external memory storage.

3. Ephemeral In-Memory Canvas & Zero Runtime Permissions

Whiteboard Simulator is engineered as a standalone, 100% self-contained offline application with zero device permissions:

  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: No dangerous or sensitive Android permissions are declared in the manifest or requested at runtime. Storage permissions are deliberately omitted because drawing strokes exist only temporarily in volatile device RAM while active.
  • 100% Offline Functionality: All drawing engines, canvas procedural shaders, sound effects, and UI components operate completely locally on the device without network dependencies or cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Advertising

Whiteboard Simulator contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, profiling trackers, or telemetry analytics tools. There are no advertising banners, interstitial videos, or data-collection SDKs embedded in the app. We never sell, lease, or monetize user data with commercial data brokers or marketing firms.

5. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

Whiteboard Simulator complies strictly with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the app is completely ad-free, collects zero data, requires no device permissions, and offers a harmless creative drawing environment, it is suitable and safe for users of all ages, including children and families.

6. Security & Data Safety

Because the app functions strictly on-device with zero internet connectivity and no external cloud database, there is zero risk of data breaches, remote leakage, or unauthorized digital profiling. The app operates safely within Android's protected application sandbox.

7. Policy Modifications

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with Google Play Store standards. Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated effective date.
