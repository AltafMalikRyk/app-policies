PopcornSimulator

Official Privacy Policy • Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Welcome to PopcornSimulator. This Privacy Policy details our unwavering commitment to protecting your digital privacy, adhering to global data protection regulations, and explaining why our application operates entirely offline with zero personal data collection.

🎭 1. Entertainment & Simulation Disclaimer

PopcornSimulator is an entertainment and sensory ASMR application developed purely for casual amusement, cinema concession nostalgia, and visual relaxation.

All elements represented within the software — including heating kettle temperatures, kernel burst physics, bubbling oil sounds, and virtual popcorn snacking interactions — are simulated digitally on your device. The application does not control any physical culinary hardware, does not produce real food, and poses zero hazard to the user or device.

🛡️ 2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe in total privacy by design. When you install and play PopcornSimulator, we collect absolutely no personal data. Specifically:

  • No names, usernames, email addresses, or phone numbers are requested or stored.
  • No device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID, or MAC address) are gathered.
  • No precise or approximate geographic location data is accessed or processed.
  • No biometric, health, financial, or behavioral information is tracked.

🔒 3. Zero Runtime & Sensitive Device Permissions

Unlike many modern mobile applications, PopcornSimulator requires zero dangerous or sensitive runtime device permissions:

  • NO Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA)
  • NO Microphone Access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)
  • NO Storage or Photo Gallery Access (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
  • NO Location Access (ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION)
  • NO Contacts, Calendar, or SMS Permissions

The application utilizes only standard device vibration hardware for tactile popping haptics (VIBRATE), which operates strictly locally on your handset.

📶 4. 100% Offline Architecture & No Server Transmission

PopcornSimulator is built as an entirely self-contained, offline application. All graphical assets, custom audio synthesizers, and physics simulations are packaged locally within the application bundle.

Offline Integrity Guarantee

The app functions without an active internet connection. It does not transmit telemetry, analytics, crash logs, or user usage habits to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.

👶 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because PopcornSimulator contains zero data collection, zero third-party advertising, and zero in-app purchases, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K), and Google Play’s Families Policy.

Parents and guardians can rest assured that children of all ages can enjoy the colorful popcorn popping simulation without any exposure to behavioral profiling, trackers, or commercial solicitations.

🚫 6. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Advertising SDKs

Our codebase is completely free of third-party advertising networks, analytics engines (such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust, or AppsFlyer), and tracking beacons. We do not monetize user data in any manner whatsoever.

💾 7. Local Storage of User Preferences

Any customization choices made within the Settings screen — such as audio volume, haptic feedback toggle, popping speed mode, and your session pop counter — are stored exclusively in local sandbox memory on your physical Android device. These settings never leave your handset and are instantly cleared if you choose to uninstall the application.
